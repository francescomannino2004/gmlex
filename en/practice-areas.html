Lending and debt recovery

A commercial and financial approach: containing costs, optimising timeframes.

This is the firm's main area of practice, alongside leading national and international banks and credit management companies: management and recovery of secured and unsecured claims, advice on credit guarantees and due diligence on assets to be securitised or acquired.

  • Out-of-court and judicial debt recovery
  • Secured and unsecured claims
  • Payment orders and enforcement against movable and real property
  • Advice on credit guarantees
  • Securitisation and asset management
  • Due diligence on receivables to be securitised or acquired

Enforcement and insolvency proceedings

Experience gained also through appointments by courts and public authorities.

Avv. Mannino acts as custodian and delegate for judicial sales in real estate enforcement proceedings, is counsel to bankruptcy trustees and companies in liquidation, and has served as liquidator of cooperative societies.

  • Custody and delegated judicial sales
  • Real estate enforcement proceedings
  • Counsel to bankruptcy trustees
  • Companies in liquidation
  • Liquidation of cooperative societies

Social security and employment

Alongside social security institutions, professionals and workers.

Avv. Mannino acts for social security institutions, including in the recovery of contribution claims, and handles social security and employment disputes before the Labour Court.

  • Counsel to social security institutions
  • Recovery of contribution claims
  • Social security litigation
  • Employment disputes
  • Fees of contracted doctors

Civil liability and compensation

Protecting those who have suffered harm.

Assistance in compensation claims, including against public authorities, as in cases of infection through blood transfusions, and in disputes over personal injury and property damage.

  • Healthcare liability
  • Damage from blood transfusions
  • Liability of public authorities
  • Damage from building collapse
  • Pecuniary and non-pecuniary damage

Administrative law

Supporting public bodies and private parties in their dealings with public administration.

The firm provides advice and litigation services in civil and administrative matters to local authorities, publicly relevant companies and private clients.

  • Advice to local authorities
  • Administrative litigation
  • Publicly relevant companies
  • Challenges to administrative penalties

Aviation law

A specialist field, alongside airlines.

Advice and litigation for Italian and foreign airlines on contractual and non-contractual issues relating to air transport.

  • Air transport contracts
  • Contractual and non-contractual liability
  • Aviation litigation

Family law

Delicate moments that call for expertise and sensitivity.

The firm assists clients in disputes and agreements concerning family relationships, favouring, wherever possible, shared solutions that protect everyone's interests, starting with those of the children, with full respect for confidentiality.

  • Consensual and judicial separation
  • Divorce
  • Child custody and residence
  • Child and spousal maintenance
  • Property relations between spouses
  • Variation of separation and divorce terms

Real estate law

Assistance at every stage in the life of a property.

From negotiation to dispute resolution, the firm supports owners, tenants, condominiums and businesses in all property-related matters.

  • Sales and preliminary contracts
  • Residential and commercial leases
  • Evictions for arrears and lease expiry
  • Condominium disputes
  • Property rights, easements and partitions
  • Protection of possession

Corporate law

Supporting businesses from incorporation to dispute resolution.

Ongoing advice and litigation support for companies and their shareholders, with a practical approach focused on the goals of the business.

  • Incorporation and drafting of articles
  • Shareholders' agreements
  • Relations and disputes between shareholders
  • Directors' liability
  • Challenges to shareholders' resolutions
  • Corporate litigation

Tax law

Defending taxpayers with rigour and expertise.

Assistance to individuals and businesses in their dealings with the tax authorities and the collection agency, from the review of the measure to proceedings before the Tax Courts.

  • Tax assessments
  • Payment notices and demands
  • First and second instance appeals
  • Pre-litigation settlement procedures
  • Suspension of tax measures

Inheritance law

Protecting assets and wishes, from one generation to the next.

The firm guides families in planning generational transfers and assists them when disputes arise among heirs, with balance and discretion.

  • Drafting of wills
  • Generational transfer planning
  • Division of estates
  • Actions to protect forced heirs
  • Challenges to wills
  • Disputes among co-heirs

Human rights and the ECHR

Fundamental rights, beyond national borders.

Experience with the European Convention for the Protection of Human Rights and Fundamental Freedoms, concerning the application of European law in Italy and protection against violations of the Convention.

  • Protection against ECHR violations
  • Application of European law in Italy
  • Fundamental rights and freedoms
