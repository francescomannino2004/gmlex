Valuation class: startup cases using a 0 to 500 per-category method (Berkus), then the scorecard method with worked examples.

### Per-category valuation method and Arditia Lab case
- Case slides are old: judge each case in its own era; solutions that came later don't count.
- Arditia Lab, Boston AI coding agent: idea strong (30 buyer interviews, 70% would pay 6,000 a month).
    - Prototype 100 to 200 (mock-up only); management near 0 (part-time founder, no co-founders)
    - Strategic relationships 350 to 400 (listed partner, framework contract); rollout around 250
- After scoring all categories, rebalance: be more generous where others compensate, less where a score was weak.

### Cobalto System (warehouse robots)
- Idea about 450: urgent validated problem, but willingness to pay unknown and competition is heavy.
- Prototype about 450: working product tested, no patent in a competitive, easily copied space.
- Management 400 to 450: experienced team; open sales role not worrying at angel stage.
- Strategic relationships 400 (350 to 450 range): partnership signed, no investor ties; rollout 350 to 400 with paid pilots.

### Baby monitoring startup
- Patented noise detection lets parents monitor babies and compare sleep patterns; free app plus paid subscription and device.
- Check that the upfront device sale alone covers product, acquisition and fixed costs; subscription is then upside.
- Scores: idea 500, product 500, team 300 (benefit of doubt), strategic 100, rollout 200 (no sales plan).
- **Exam questions may have more than one correct answer, and this won't be stated.**

### Scorecard method
- Step one: average pre-money valuation of comparable local deals (screen by geography, stage) as baseline.
- Step two: weighted adjustment factor, each category scored 0 to 2 (1 = average).
    - Team 30%, opportunity size 25%, product/technology 15%, competition 10%, sales 10%, funding needs 5%, other 5%
    - Fierce competition lowers the score
- Multiply each score by its weight, sum, then multiply baseline (example: 10 million × 1.5).
- Follow-on rounds at fast rising valuations (e.g. 330 million to 900 million in months) are a current hype trend.

### Scorecard exercises
- San Mateo cloud developer tool, serial founders: baseline 6 million, factor 1.68, valuation 10.08 million.
- Cross-platform app platform: baseline 12 million, factor around 1.76.
    - Team 2, market about 2, product 1.6, competition 1.2, sales 1.7, funding 1.5, other 1.2
- Factor summation method is the same idea with more factors (management, stage, reputation, etc.).
