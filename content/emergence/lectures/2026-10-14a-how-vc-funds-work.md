Lecture on how venture capital funds work (fundraising, management fees, carry, exits, follow-ons, LPs) plus European and Italian VC statistics.

### Networking Events
- Value of events depends on the quality of the network
- Slush in Helsinki (November) recommended as a top international VC event

### How VC Funds Work
- VCs must fundraise from LPs before investing; the speaker is currently raising a new fund and calls it a massive pain
- Cycle: raise, scout deals, negotiate term sheets, manage portfolio, exit; good funds bring 'smart money', crowdfunding is classic 'dumb money'
- Exits are a full sale or selling secondary shares; secondaries typically sold at a 15 to 20 percent discount
    - Speaker's funds had three exits; two secondaries at 4.5x to 5.5x, forced by lack of follow-on capital
- Seed funds deploy capital in 2 to 3 years; venture or growth funds take 6 to 7 years

### Fund Economics: 2 and 20
- Management fees of about 2% of AUM per year cover operations; carry is 20% of profits after returning LP capital plus hurdle rate
- Partners and principals typically put in 1 to 2% of the fund themselves, sometimes via loans, to access carry
- Classic seed split: 55 to 60 percent new investments, 40 to 45 percent follow-ons into winners
    - Follow-ons are where funds make most of their money, hence the push for hockey-stick growth
- Example fund (Partech) grew from about 40 to 500 million AUM with 12 to 13 people to about 3.5 billion with 60 people

### LPs, Deal Flow and Founder Advice
- LP types: institutional (e.g. Cassa Depositi e Prestiti), pension funds, family offices, corporates seeking tech and acquisition targets
- Italian pension funds rarely invest in VC due to tax framework and weak exit track record
- Funds say no 98 to 99% of the time: about 3,000 to 4,000 deals seen, 30 to 40 investments per year
- Judge VCs by how quickly and clearly they say no; avoid giving away too much equity out of desperation

### European and Italian VC Landscape
- Europe 2025: 69 billion, 46% below 2021 and 3.4 times smaller than the US; 61% linked to AI/ML
- Italy 2025: 1.7 billion over 434 rounds, 80% seed and pre-seed, versus UK 10 to 11 billion, France about 8 billion, Spain 3.5 billion
- Italy 2026: 1.3 billion by end of September, skewed by one 234 million round; expected 1.8 to 1.9 billion
- International investors in 65% of amount but only 30% of rounds; Italy needs stronger deal flow to attract them
