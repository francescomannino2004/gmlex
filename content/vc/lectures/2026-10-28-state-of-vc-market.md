### State of the VC Market (Q3 2026)
- Global VC was 171 billion, down 28.5% vs prior quarter but up 43% vs last year; prior quarter boosted by OpenAI and Anthropic rounds.
- AI takes 64% of funding (82 to 83% year to date), an unprecedented concentration; 27 rounds above 1 billion.
    - Spray-and-pray still fits AI apps, but megarounds force bigger, more hands-on bets
    - 'Pre-team' investing in top lab talent before any idea
- 75% of US capital went to 5% of deals: an extreme power law; data providers differ due to no disclosure mandate in private capital.
- LPs put more into VC than they get back; 88% of capital goes to experienced managers, signaling uncertainty.

### IPOs, Bubble Debate and Regions
- A company only receives the IPO primary price; a big day-one jump means underpricing by the bank, not success.
- Class debated whether a heavily indebted AI cloud company will IPO in November or postpone again.
- Bubble debate illustrated with Amazon falling to $6 in dot-com crash; Speaker 1 doubts LLMs will be the main AI paradigm.
- US, Europe and China are similar in size; Israel, fourth, is about 10 times smaller; VC over GDP is a better comparison.
