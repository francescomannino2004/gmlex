MISSION
The course develops student’s understanding of how firms use technology and innovation to
position themselves strategically, from the perspective of a Director of R&D, Corporate
Development, and Corporate Venturing. It pays specific attention to the AI transformation which
is impacting all industries in the recent years. For this purpose, it relies heavily on cases and
examples, dealing with firms which have faced the challenges of managing innovation,
technology strategy, and the digital transformation. Students are asked to actively participate in
class discussions to confront the implications of technology and innovation on a firm’s strategy
and to understand actions and reactions by Executives of Business Development when
technology and innovation originate within the firm as well as when they change exogenously
the environment where the firm operates.

ATTENDING STUDENTS
The final grade will be computed as follows:
Class participation (30%) will be assessed at the group level. Students are expected to
form their own groups of up to six members. Each group should choose a name and
appoint a spokesperson, to whom the instructors will address any group-specific
communications. The group’s name and list of members, including the spokesperson,
should be submitted by noon on Friday, September 11, via email
to giulia.corbella@unibocconi.it. The assessment of class participation will be based on
both groups’ voluntary contributions and their responses to cold calls during case
discussions.
The group work (20%) will test students’ ability to apply the concepts covered in class to
a practical context. Working in self-selected groups, students will analyze how a real-
world company has successfully leveraged Corporate Venturing and develop a clear
understanding of the factors underlying its success.
A final written exam (50%) will evaluate students’ overall understanding of the course
content.
To qualify as attending students, students must attend at least 75% of classes (attendance will
be recorded at each session) and participate in the group project.
Attending-student status will remain valid for the December 2026 and January 2027
examination sessions.
NOT-ATTENDING STUDENTS
The assessment of non-attending students is based entirely on a final exam. The exam
evaluates students’ understanding of innovation, technological change and their management,
as well as their ability to think critically and apply the models covered in the course to business
situations involving technology and innovation.
