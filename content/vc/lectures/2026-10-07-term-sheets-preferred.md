VC lecture recapping the moral hazard financing model, then covering term sheets, pre/post-money valuation, and preferred stock features (liquidation preference, redemption, conversion, participation); a new case session on a CEO dispute began.

### Moral Hazard Model Recap
- Investor rationality constraint: expected return to lender, PH(R − R_B), must be at least I − A.
- Required entrepreneur assets rise with project cost and private benefit B, fall with return; PH equal to PL means no one lends.
    - Weaker, slower courts (Europe vs US) raise B, so Europe demands more collateral
- With zero payoff on failure, debt vs equity is irrelevant; with leftover value, debt protects the investor.
- Double moral hazard (entrepreneur and VC both need incentives) points to equity rather than plain debt.

### Term Sheets and Valuation
- Term sheet is a preliminary agreement; bargaining power shifts with market cycles (VCs held power in the 2008 credit crunch).
- Post-money = pre-money + investment; example: 8 million pre, 2 million invested, 10 million post, investor share 20%.
    - Analysts quote post-money; investors think in pre-money then add the investment
- Differing national laws hinder cross-border VC; EU's proposed '28th regime' and US Delaware incorporation mentioned.
- Contracts also cover anti-dilution, covenants and conditional terms, and employee terms like 16-month non-competes.

### Preferred Stock Features
- VCs use preferred stock, paid before common, giving some downside protection without being debt.
- Liquidation preference multiplier: 95.8% of US deals in Q2 2026 used 1x; higher multiples squeeze founders.
- Unconditional redemption rights signal strong investor power and can force liquidation; only 5.4% of deals used them.
- Participating preferred lets investors also share in the founders' residual; 96.4% of deals were non-participating.

### Convertible Preferred Example
- 2 million at 5 dollars per share gives 400k shares; with 600k existing, investor owns 40%.
- Convert when 40% of exit value exceeds 2 million, so above 5 million; a 2x multiple shifts the threshold right.
- Asking for convertibility implies the investor expects post-money of at least 5 million, so pre-money 3 million.
- Caps can limit conversion upside; these terms push VCs to add value toward higher valuations.
