Guest lecture by a 360 Capital partner (former student of the course) on her VC career, portfolio deals from HR tech to deep tech, Europe vs US funding gaps, and breaking into VC.

### Course Logistics and Speaker Background
- Case posted online is not compulsory for the exam but useful for reviewing contractual clauses.
- Speaker 2 is a former student (graduated 2019), did consulting, joined 360 Capital in 2020, became partner 2 years ago.
- Core VC skill is sourcing: one company can return the whole fund (power law).

### Portfolio Examples
- Skillview: AI interview assessment, 6 million seed, founder was 25; SAP just bought competitor Tech Wolf.
    - HR seen as easiest AI entry point; cybersecurity more critical but slow compliance-heavy sales
- Thunder: employee benefits on a debit card, Italian copycat of models in France and Spain; copycats still viable in Italy.
- Cortech (Polimi spin-off) optimizes wearable chip battery life; high risk, high reward, 7 to 10 expert touch points pre-term sheet.
    - Patents can reveal claims; ring-fenced strategy with decoy patents suggested
- Other deals: wastewater metal recovery firm (EU CRMA strategic project), Cybercode (AI agent detection), Energy Dome (CO2 energy storage).

### Europe vs US Ecosystem
- Energy Dome is flipping to the US: next round 300 million is beyond European investors, main clients are abroad.
- Europe lacks large funds and pension allocation (0.12%); Italian pension funds invest mainly for tax incentives.
- Average exit value in Europe cited as 51 million; Anthropic Series H rivals all European VC in 2025.
- Discussed 28th regime, stigma of failure in Italy, and SME mindset limiting founder ambition.

### AI Models and Being a Useful VC
- Future is choosing models per task; cheaper open or niche models for repetitive work, top models for complex tasks.
- VC should be hands-on like a doctor: available when founders ask, never dictating decisions.

### Breaking Into VC
- Interviews focus on motivation, knowledge of the fund's portfolio, opinions on trends, and a live pitch deck case without AI.
- Recommended reading: TechCrunch, Sifted, MIT Tech Review, Financial Times.
- Partnership requires personal capital at stake; Speaker 2 took a loan to invest.
